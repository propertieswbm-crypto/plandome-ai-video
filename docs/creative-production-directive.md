# Creative Production Directive

Every advertisement is composed as original agency work rather than selected
from one fixed template. Before rendering, the pipeline creates a seeded Design
DNA covering creative direction, layout, motion, type, camera, transitions,
colour, texture, depth, lighting, graphics, overlays, CTA treatment, easing,
spacing and visual rhythm.

Templates are modular production components. Each scene receives a compatible
recipe such as a hero with floating cards and kinetic typography, a dashboard
with charts and statistics, or a split screen with feature cards and animated
icons. Components are adapted to the current Design DNA.

The product remains visually dominant. Scenes must introduce a new composition,
and the pre-render review rejects empty, shallow or repeated scene recipes.
Every exported composition records its Design DNA, component recipes, feature
coverage and review result in `DESIGN.md` and in the composition runtime.
